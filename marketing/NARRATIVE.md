# Why Shorebound exists

The origin story, the reader we're for, and the voice. Everything written
under this name — the home page, the landing page, app store copy, a Reddit
post, a pitch to a bait shop — comes from here.

**Two things are marked `[OWNER]`.** They are the personal facts only you can
supply, and they are deliberately blank rather than invented. Everything else
is true and checkable today.

---

## The reader

**You already know how to fish. You just don't know this water.**

That is the whole thing, and it is the sentence every other line has to earn.

He has twenty years on him. He can read a lake at a glance — where the bottom
changes, where the shade line falls, what a wind out of the north does to a
point. He owns rods he has opinions about. He has taught someone else to cast.

Then he stands on a Florida beach with a pass ripping out in front of him and
**none of it transfers.** The water moves twice a day and that turns out to be
the whole game. The structure is oyster and mangrove and bridge piling instead
of timber and weed line. The fish are different fish. Several of them will hurt
him and nobody has mentioned which.

He is not a beginner. He is displaced. **Those need completely different
tools**, and everything on the market is built for the first one.

### What he does not want

**To pay someone else to figure it out.** He could book a charter. Plenty of
people should, and it is a good day out. But he does not want to be handed a
rod on somebody else's boat, over somebody else's spot, on somebody else's
plan. The catching is not the point. **Working it out is the point** — that is
the part he actually likes, and buying it removes it.

`[OWNER]` — *one or two sentences on the specific moment this became personal.
The trip that was wasted, the spot an app sent you to, the thing an old-timer
told you in ten seconds that no app knew. This is the only part of the story
that cannot be researched, and it is the part people will remember.*

## What it does, in his order

Not a feature list. The order a person actually asks these in, standing in a
car park at half five in the morning.

1. **Where do I go?** Twenty-five researched spots, St. Petersburg to Boca
   Grande Pass. Twenty-two reachable on foot — beach, pier, bridge, flat — so
   he does not need a boat to start.
2. **When?** Tide stage is the answer, and it is the thing freshwater never
   taught him. Every spot says the stage it actually fishes, against a live
   NOAA prediction for its own station.
3. **What am I standing over?** Grass, oyster bar, mangrove edge, bridge
   piling, a pass. What each one is, and why fish sit on it.
4. **What will I catch?** The species that spot actually holds, not a regional
   list. Snook, redfish, trout, tarpon, sheepshead, mackerel.
5. **What do I throw at it?** Rig, hook, leader, weight and bait — per species,
   per spot. Not "use live bait." *Free-line a pilchard on a 3/0 with 30 lb
   fluoro, because the current here is doing this.*
6. **What do I do when it's on the sand?** — and this is the one nobody
   prepares him for.

### The one that matters most

**Six of these fish will hurt you.**

A hardhead catfish has a serrated spine with venom on it and people pick them
up bare-handed every single day. A snook's gill plate is a razor and it will
open your hand while you are being careful. Stingrays are under the sand you
are standing on. This is not scaremongering — it is Tuesday, and it is the
single most useful thing a visiting angler can be told before it happens
rather than after.

So handling comes first, not last: how to hold it, where not to put your
fingers, and how to put it back alive. **That content is free forever**, for
anyone, paid or not. There is a test in the codebase that fails the build if
anyone ever changes that.

`[OWNER]` — *who taught you, if anyone did. One line.*

## Why it is not just another fishing app

Fishbrain has seventy thousand ratings. Fishbox has thirty-seven thousand.
They will all hand you a number — a bite score, a coloured dial — and none of
them will tell you why.

They cannot. An app covering every water in the country has no room for what
matters on one three-mile stretch: which bank goes slack first, which pass
turns into a washing machine on the outgoing, which bridge fishes at night and
which is a wasted evening. Local knowledge does not scale to fifty states, so
it gets replaced by a model output.

**Every competitor gives you a score. This one shows its work.**

### And the local part is a maintenance job, not a feature

In 2024, Helene and Milton took this coast apart.

Annie's Bait & Tackle in Cortez — flooded, condemned, demolished at sunrise on
16 April 2025. **Annie's website is still live. It still publishes opening
hours.** Anna Maria City Pier lost four hundred feet of walkway. The Rod & Reel
Pier is pilings and a sign. Stump Pass has a new pass through it that Milton
cut, and the mile-and-a-third walk to the tip does not exist any more.

You will not find any of that in a national app. Their data still says those
places are open, because nobody drove out to look.

## What we will not do

Rules the code enforces, not marketing promises. They are readable in the
source.

**We do not invent fishing content.** If nobody has researched a spot's
seasons, that field is empty. Empty means "not done yet", never "nothing to
say" — and we would rather show the gap than fill it with something plausible.

**We do not claim to predict a catch.** There is no catch data behind this
app, so a likelihood number would be unfalsifiable. What we will tell you is
which spot matches the water right now and exactly why — this tide stage, this
hour, this month, and the researched note saying why it fishes then.

**We do not hide safety behind a paywall.** See above. Permanently.

**Your location never leaves your phone.** Ranking spots by distance is
arithmetic against data already on the device. No request in this app carries
a coordinate. The photo identifier never stores a picture, anywhere.

**Paid placement always says it is paid.** Local shops can pay to stand out in
the bait directory. They cannot pay to be *in* it, and they cannot pay a
competitor out of it.

## Voice

**Plain, specific, unhurried.** He has been sold to by fishing apps before and
can smell it. He is also competent — write to an equal who is missing local
knowledge, never to a novice.

- **Concrete beats enthusiastic.** "Longboat Pass, outgoing through dawn"
  beats "unlock your best day on the water." Every time.
- **Name the limits.** Saying what we do not know is the reason to believe
  what we do. It is the most persuasive thing available and it costs nothing
  but discipline.
- **Never condescend.** He knows what a leader is. He does not know what the
  tide does to this pass.
- **No hype, no fake urgency, no emoji strings, no invented testimonials.**
- **Sound like an angler, not a brand.** If a sentence could appear in any
  app's marketing, delete it.
